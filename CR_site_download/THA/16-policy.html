16.1 Evolution of National Development Strategy

ASEAN comparative lens (2026). Thailand should be read in ASEAN comparison as an upper-middle-income mainland hub shaped by tourism, automotive manufacturing, civil-military politics, and rapid aging. It differs from Vietnam's newer FDI-led industrial acceleration, Indonesia's scale-driven domestic market, Singapore's high-capacity city-state, and Cambodia-Laos resource/corridor models because its central challenge is upgrading a mature but politically constrained middle-income economy. Thailand's policy strategy has evolved from import substitution and export industrialization to post-1997 stabilization, sufficiency-economy framing, Thailand 4.0, the 20-Year National Strategy, the 13th Plan, BCG economy, EEC, digital government, and green manufacturing.
