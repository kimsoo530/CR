ASEAN comparative lens (2026). Thailand should be read in ASEAN comparison as an upper-middle-income mainland hub shaped by tourism, automotive manufacturing, civil-military politics, and rapid aging. It differs from Vietnam's newer FDI-led industrial acceleration, Indonesia's scale-driven domestic market, Singapore's high-capacity city-state, and Cambodia-Laos resource/corridor models because its central challenge is upgrading a mature but politically constrained middle-income economy.
