11. Infrastructure, Energy, and Digital Connectivity

Near-universal access shifts the energy question from connection to reliability, generation mix, grid investment, and industrial power security.
Digital connectivity gives Vietnam a platform for online services, e-identification, and data integration; it also raises new questions about privacy, cybersecurity, and administrative interoperability.
Logistics is a governance variable in Vietnam: export competitiveness depends on ports, customs, provincial land administration, road corridors, and predictable regulation.
