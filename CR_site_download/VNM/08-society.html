8. Population and Social Structure

Vietnam's demographic scale creates both administrative reach and economic opportunity. The policy problem is changing quickly from absorbing a young labor force to adapting public finance, pensions, health care, and local services to population aging.
Urbanization, aging, and the shrinking youth share point in the same administrative direction: local governments will face more complex demand for housing, transport, social insurance, primary health care, and elderly care.
