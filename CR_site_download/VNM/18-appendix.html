18.6 Statistical Country Profile

The statistical appendix compares Viet Nam with a fixed ASEAN peer group—Indonesia, the Philippines, Thailand, and Malaysia—across demographic transition, macro-fiscal conditions, production and trade, labor and human capital, infrastructure, environmental pressure, governance, and cabinet organization. The comparisons are descriptive benchmarks rather than causal counterfactuals.

The appendix visualizes all 105 indicators available for Viet Nam in the validated run. Multi-country trends use both color and line style, Internet use is shown as a ten-year country trend, and every figure provides hover-accessible country-year values. Future-period IMF and UN values are explicitly identified as projections or values derived from projected inputs.
