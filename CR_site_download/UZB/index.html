GDI Country Studies:
Uzbekistan

Understanding the State, Institutions, Economy, Society, and Strategic Position of Uzbekistan

This book is designed to help readers understand a country as an operating state system. It explains the institutional framework through official laws and government documents, tests performance through international indicators, and incorporates international diagnostic reports and academic debates where they sharpen the analysis.
