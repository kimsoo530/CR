Mongolia

Understanding Mongolia's Democratic State, Mineral Economy, Steppe Society, Administrative Capacity, and Strategic Position

This electronic book is organized around a Mongolia-specific thesis: a democratic, resource-rich, landlocked state must convert mineral wealth into resilient institutions, diversified growth, and services that work across a vast low-density territory.
