10. Trade, Investment, and External Economy

Trade openness reflects Cambodia's integration through garments, footwear, travel goods, agriculture, tourism, and SEZ manufacturing. It also exposes the economy to tariff, logistics, border, and rules-of-origin risks.
The current account helps identify whether exports, tourism receipts, remittances, and imports for construction and investment are moving in a sustainable direction.
FDI is central to SEZs, real estate, infrastructure, and manufacturing; the policy issue is whether it generates domestic linkages and better jobs.
The GDP-relative view prevents over-reading nominal FDI gains when the economy itself is growing or when investment is concentrated in low-linkage sectors.
