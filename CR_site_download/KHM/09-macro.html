9. Macroeconomic Structure

Cambodia's growth record is strong but vulnerable to shocks because it depends heavily on garments, tourism, construction, agriculture, credit, and external demand.
Rising income has reduced poverty, but reaching upper-middle-income status requires productivity, human capital, and more formal, diversified firms.
