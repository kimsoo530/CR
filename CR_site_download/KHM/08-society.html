8. Population and Social Structure

Cambodia's young population remains a development asset, but the administrative challenge is converting youth cohorts into skilled, protected, and productive workers rather than precarious labor migrants.
Urbanization around Phnom Penh, border towns, tourism corridors, and SEZs creates demand for land management, infrastructure, housing, skills, and municipal finance.
