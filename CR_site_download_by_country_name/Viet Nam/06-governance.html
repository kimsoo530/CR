6. Governance and Institutional Capacity

The six governance series should be read as institutional symptoms rather than a scoreboard. Vietnam's strongest comparative asset is political stability; the harder institutional test is whether rule-of-law, voice, and corruption-control constraints can be relaxed without weakening the coordination capacity that has supported industrialization.
