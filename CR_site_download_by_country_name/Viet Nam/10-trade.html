10. Trade, Investment, and External Economy

Trade openness is the most visible expression of Vietnam's development model. It also means that customs administration, standards, logistics, industrial zones, and diplomatic risk management are core state-capacity functions.
Current-account movements help distinguish resilient export competitiveness from periods of import-intensive investment or global demand stress.
The absolute FDI series shows the scale of external capital embedded in Vietnam's manufacturing transformation.
The GDP-relative series prevents a common interpretive error: a rising dollar value can coexist with a falling macroeconomic weight as the domestic economy expands.
