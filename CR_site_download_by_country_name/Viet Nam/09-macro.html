9. Macroeconomic Structure

The pattern captures Vietnam's policy achievement and its vulnerability. Fast growth has been compatible with macro stability, but the export-led model is sensitive to trade policy shocks, credit cycles, and the quality of public investment.
Rising income per person marks Vietnam's movement toward upper-middle-income status. The next threshold depends less on labor-cost advantages and more on productivity, technology transfer, skills, and domestic-firm upgrading.
