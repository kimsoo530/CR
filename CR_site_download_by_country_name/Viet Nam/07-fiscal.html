7. Fiscal System and Public Sector

Vietnam's fiscal profile is more than a question of aggregate prudence. The central issue is whether revenue mobilization, public investment execution, and recurrent spending discipline can support infrastructure, social protection, and provincial service delivery at the same time.
Debt ratios alone understate the fiscal management challenge because public investment, SOE restructuring, and banking-sector risk can move liabilities across institutional boundaries. The chart is therefore paired with text on budget law, audit, and quasi-fiscal exposure.
