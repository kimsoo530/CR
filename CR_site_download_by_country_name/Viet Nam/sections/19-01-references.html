ASEAN comparative lens (2026). Vietnam should be read in ASEAN comparison as ASEAN's most prominent party-led, export-manufacturing upgrading case. Unlike the Philippines' service-remittance profile, Thailand's older industrial-tourism base, Malaysia's resource-industrial federal structure, or Indonesia's archipelagic domestic-market scale, Vietnam's comparative issue is how a socialist party-state manages very open global production networks.
