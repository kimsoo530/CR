16.1 Evolution of National Development Strategy

ASEAN comparative lens (2026). Indonesia should be read in ASEAN comparison as ASEAN's largest territorial-population case and largest domestic-market state. Unlike Singapore's city-state model, Malaysia's federalized diversity, Vietnam's party-led manufacturing state, or Thailand's tourism-automotive middle-income model, Indonesia's distinctive issue is whether democratic decentralization and presidential coordination can govern a very large archipelago at continental scale. For this section, the regional comparison should test national development plans against ASEAN alternatives: industrial upgrading, green transition, digital government, corridor development, and human-capital strategy.
