11. Infrastructure, Energy, and Digital Connectivity

The policy frontier has moved from connection to affordability, reliability, grid planning, and clean power for industry.
Digital connectivity can improve public services and finance, but government systems remain fragmented unless data governance and shared infrastructure improve.
Logistics quality is central to export diversification because Cambodian firms face high costs moving goods across borders, ports, and domestic corridors.
