7. Fiscal System and Public Sector

Fiscal capacity is one of Cambodia's decisive state-building tests. Revenue reform has to finance health, education, infrastructure, social protection, and sub-national services while preserving buffers against external shocks.
Low public debt gives Cambodia room to maneuver, but debt indicators do not capture all risks. Real-estate stress, credit vulnerabilities, contingent liabilities, and project quality matter for fiscal resilience.
