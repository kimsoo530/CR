6. Governance and Institutional Capacity

Cambodia's governance profile should be read through the coexistence of post-conflict state reconstruction, dominant-party stability, and persistent rule-of-law and corruption-control constraints. Stability has supported investment and public-service expansion, but institutional predictability remains a central reform issue.
