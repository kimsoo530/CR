13. Education, Health, and Welfare

The upper-secondary and tertiary pipeline is a binding constraint on Cambodia's ambition to diversify beyond low-wage manufacturing.
Health improvements since the post-conflict period are substantial, but nutrition, primary care, social protection, and regional access remain central state-capacity tests.
