Price stability determines whether growth can be converted into real purchasing power and credible public budgeting. In Myanmar, inflation, exchange-rate movement, and reserve cover should be read as a single stability system: inflation affects real wages and procurement costs; exchange-rate depreciation changes import prices, external debt service, and remittance values; reserves indicate how much external pressure can be absorbed before adjustment becomes abrupt.
