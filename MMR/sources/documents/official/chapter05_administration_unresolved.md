# Chapter 5 Administrative Evidence: Unresolved Items

This note records gaps that remain open after the focused local source collection on 2026-08-27.

- A current consolidated English Civil Services Personnel Law with the 2016 and 2018 amendments was not locally acquired. The 2013 original is verified; amendment metadata was visible in an official SLRD landing-page result, but the PDF endpoint returned a redirect loop and is not registered as a local source.
- The underlying text of SAC Order No. 119/2021, which the GAD website cites for the 5 May 2021 transfer back to the Ministry of Home Affairs, was not acquired. This remains a verified official GAD claim, not a fully verified order text.
- A Myanmar Digital News page reproducing Order No. 119/2021 was located during focused search, but the official host could not be locally preserved because of TLS/403 access failures. It was therefore not added to the source register and does not change the local-file verification status.
- The complete legal instrument for the 28 December 2018 GAD transfer was not acquired. The date and institutional change are supported by GAD's official history, while the Asia Foundation and 2014 GAD study provide historical context.
- Neither the 2014 GAD study nor the 2019 Asia Foundation report establishes current nationwide administrative reach after the 2021 coup and subsequent conflict.
- Current staffing levels, vacancies, resignations, CDM effects, recruitment, and personnel deployment are not sufficiently documented in the acquired primary files for a nationwide claim.
- NUG-associated, ethnic-governance, and other parallel administrative systems require dated, actor-specific and independent evidence. No precise territorial-control claim or current geography should be added to Chapter 5 from the present package.
- Current service delivery, taxation, dispute-resolution and education/health administration require separate sector evidence and should not be inferred from GAD's formal mandate.
