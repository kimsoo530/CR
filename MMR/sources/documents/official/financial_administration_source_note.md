# Financial Administration Source Note: Myanmar

This note records the official and international source types required for section 5.4.

Official source checklist:

- Constitution and budget law or organic public finance law.
- Annual budget proposal, enacted budget or appropriation act, budget speech, and medium-term fiscal framework.
- Finance ministry, treasury, budget office, revenue authority, and debt office budget execution tables.
- Parliamentary budget committee documents and budget review reports.
- Final accounts, consolidated financial statements, and year-end budget execution reports.
- Supreme audit institution, auditor-general, court of accounts, or audit chamber annual reports.
- Procurement law, procurement authority reports, e-procurement data, contract award data, and inspection reports.

International source:
International Monetary Fund. (2026). IMF DataMapper API: general government revenue, expenditure, net lending/borrowing, and primary balance. https://www.imf.org/external/datamapper/api/

Current section source summary: Ministry of Planning and Finance; Auditor General of the Union; budget laws; World Bank and IMF Myanmar diagnostics.
