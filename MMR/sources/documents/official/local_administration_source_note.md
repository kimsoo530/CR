# Local Administration and Service Delivery Source Note: Myanmar

This note records the official and comparative source types required for section 5.5.

Official source checklist:

- Constitution and local-government law.
- Election law or appointment rules for governors, mayors, district heads, council chairs, and village heads.
- Ministry of finance central and local budget execution tables, including tax revenue, non-tax revenue, transfers, and expenditure by function.
- Tax code or local finance law identifying local own-source taxes and fees.
- Civil-service commission or personnel agency statistics separating central, regional, local, education, health, and other staff where available.
- Education, health, welfare, infrastructure, and economic-development ministry documents defining central and local responsibilities.
- Audit reports, parliamentary committee reports, local-government association documents, and academic studies on central-local conflict.

Comparative source:
OECD/UCLG. (2022). *World Observatory on Subnational Government Finance and Investment: Country profiles and database*. https://www.sng-wofi.org/country_profiles/presentation.html

Current section source summary: Constitution; local-government law; finance ministry local budget tables; ministry of interior/local government; civil-service or public employment statistics; OECD/UCLG SNG-WOFI country profile where available.
