## Verification method

PDF page counts and extracted title/provision text were checked locally with `pypdf`; HTML title, issuer, date/number, and body text were checked from the saved files. The SLRD Civil Service Law URL returned a redirect loop/301 during this batch, and the attempted GAD association-law URL returned 404; neither is treated as an acquired source. A focused Myanmar Digital News page reproducing Order No. 119/2021 was also opened in web search but could not be locally preserved because of TLS/403 failures, so it was not registered as a locally verified source.
