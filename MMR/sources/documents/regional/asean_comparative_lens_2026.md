# ASEAN Comparative Lens Note (2026)

## Regional Thesis

Myanmar should be interpreted not only as a national case but as part of ASEAN's internal variation in state capacity, development strategy, geography, income level, and regime form. In this regional frame, it is ASEAN's most fragile contemporary state-capacity and conflict-governance case. It differs from the developmental trajectories of Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, and Indonesia because regime conflict, armed fragmentation, sanctions, and administrative breakdown dominate the policy environment.

## Editorial Use

When writing an ASEAN country section, first identify whether the country is distinctive by population scale, income level, political settlement, territorial form, administrative capacity, development model, external dependence, or security geography. Then compare it with the most relevant ASEAN peers, not with an abstract global average. For example, Singapore is a city-state outlier; Indonesia is the scale-and-archipelago case; Vietnam is the party-led manufacturing case; Thailand is the aging middle-income hub; Malaysia is the federal upper-middle-income case; the Philippines is the archipelagic democracy and labor-migration case; Cambodia and Lao PDR are smaller externally dependent mainland cases; Myanmar is the conflict-fragility case.
