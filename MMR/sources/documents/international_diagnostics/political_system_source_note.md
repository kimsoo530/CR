# Political System Source Note: Myanmar

This note records source types required for chapter 4.

Core comparative indicators:

- V-Dem vdemdata: electoral democracy, liberal democracy, electoral integrity, clean elections, party-system institutionalization, legislative constraints, judicial constraints, rule of law, political corruption, public-sector corruption, and regime classification.
- World Bank WGI: voice and accountability, political stability, rule of law, and control of corruption.

Official and country-specific sources to archive:

- Constitution, electoral law, political-party law, parliamentary rules, campaign-finance rules, and election commission reports.
- Election results, voter turnout, candidate registration, observer reports, court decisions on electoral disputes, and party-system data.
- Judiciary council, supreme court, constitutional court, prosecutor, anti-corruption agency, audit institution, and ombudsman materials.
- Historical sources on regime formation, coups, revolutions, independence settlements, party-system change, military or security influence, and central-local political bargaining.
