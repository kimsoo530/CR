# Chapter 17 unresolved synthesis issues

- No single verified dataset measures nationwide administrative or territorial reach across all authorities.
- Chapter 7 does not support a complete current fiscal-performance assessment: the IMF expenditure raw source remains missing and official budget execution/audit materials were not verified.
- Chapter 15 does not establish a current recognition outcome for competing representatives, and no territorial-control map is supported.
- Chapter 16 does not establish MERP approval, implementation, or strategy performance; FDC is an alternative political/interim framework rather than a conventional development plan.
- Cross-chapter evidence supports mechanisms and constraints, but not an arbitrary composite governance score.
- Locality-specific service or governance evidence must not be generalized nationwide.
