## Core synthesis rule

Chapter 17 may state that formal institutional designs coexist with contested authority, uneven administrative operation, incomplete fiscal evidence, security pressure and competing strategic frameworks. It may not turn this into a claim of uniform national control, strategy failure, or a single measured level of state capacity.
