# Chapter 15 Supervisor Handoff

The original Chapter 15 consisted of four short sections dominated by repeated ASEAN-comparative boilerplate and generic lists of diplomacy, security, borders and transnational risks. It lacked verified foreign-affairs architecture, representation analysis, dated ASEAN evidence, humanitarian definitions and a clear territorial-control evidence boundary.

This batch added a verified evidence package consisting of the 2008 Constitution, ASEAN's 2021 Five-Point Consensus, ASEAN's 2025 East Asia Summit statement, UN credentials documents A/76/550 and A/RES/76/15, the 2021 Special Rapporteur report, and OCHA's 2025 HNRP. The candidate separates formal design, actor claims, observed conditions, representation, recognition and humanitarian planning estimates.

Benchmark matching used NGA for conflict/security and uneven reach framing, THA for military-politics and border-security organization, and VNM for structured foreign-relations presentation. No substantive country facts were transferred from benchmarks.

Three low-risk SVG visuals were produced: an actor-category schematic, a representation/ASEAN timeline, and an OCHA-based internal-displacement trend. No territorial-control or conflict map was produced because dated spatial evidence and methodology were insufficient.
