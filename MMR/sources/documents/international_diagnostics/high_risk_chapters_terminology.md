# High-risk chapter terminology convention

- **SAC:** State Administration Council; use for the post-2021 body and its attributed official statements.
- **CRPH:** Committee Representing Pyidaungsu Hluttaw; use for the body and its attributed instruments or statements.
- **NUG:** National Unity Government; use for NUG-associated institutions or attributed claims, without implying uncontested nationwide authority.
- **FDC:** Federal Democracy Charter; identify it as an alternative political/interim constitutional framework unless a source supports a narrower description.
- **EAO/EAOs:** ethnic armed organisation(s); use actor-specific names when evidence permits.
- **Defence Services/Tatmadaw:** use “Defence Services” for constitutional text and “Tatmadaw” for historical or analytical references where appropriate.
- **government:** reserve for an identified formal or actor-specific government; do not use as a neutral synonym for all post-2021 authority claims.
- **state:** use for the country or formal state institutions; do not equate state presence with effective reach.
- **administration:** use for organizational machinery or observed administrative activity.
- **authority:** identify whether formal, claimed, contested or observed.
- **control:** use only for dated, explicitly evidenced spatial or institutional control; do not infer it from formal boundaries.
- **representation:** participation or credentials status in an institution; it is not recognition.
- **recognition:** use only where an authoritative source makes an explicit recognition determination.
- **engagement:** diplomatic contact or participation; it is not recognition.

Evidence language: formal/de jure, official claim, observed operation, de facto observation, contested, partially supported, and unresolved.
