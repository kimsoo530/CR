# Macroeconomic Structure Source Note: Myanmar

- Retrieval country code: `MMR`.
- Core World Bank WDI table IDs: `NY.GDP.MKTP.CD, NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG, NY.GDP.PCAP.CD, FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG, NY.GDP.DEFL.KD.ZG, PA.NUS.FCRF, FI.RES.TOTL.MO, NE.EXP.GNFS.CD, NE.IMP.GNFS.CD, TX.VAL.MRCH.CD.WT, TM.VAL.MRCH.CD.WT, BN.CAB.XOKA.GD.ZS, NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS, NV.AGR.TOTL.ZS, NV.IND.TOTL.ZS, NV.SRV.TOTL.ZS`.
- Computed indicators: `CALC.GNFS.BAL.CD` is exports of goods and services minus imports of goods and services; `CALC.MRCH.BAL.CD` is merchandise exports minus merchandise imports.
- Units are kept separate in charts: current US$ values are converted to current US$ billions; percentage indicators use percent; exchange rates use local currency units per US dollar; reserves use months of imports.
- Annual update rule: triangulate WDI with the national statistical office, central bank, finance ministry, IMF Article IV, and World Bank country economic updates before publication.
