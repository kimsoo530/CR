# 20–30 minute manual review checklist

Review the six candidate files in order. For each passage, mark **accept**, **narrow**, or **hold**.

## Chapter 3 — 4 minutes

- Constitutional territorial structure and reserved military representation.
- Section 436 threshold and whether “veto” is framed as an institutional inference rather than text.
- Articles 232, 417–419 and the 1–2 February 2021 sequence.
- Final paragraph distinguishing SAC-side continuity claims from CRPH/NUG/FDC claims.

Check legal wording, official attribution and source-page alignment.

## Chapter 5 — 4 minutes

- GAD institutional history and reference period.
- Formal Region/State–district–township–ward/village-tract chain.
- Civil-service rules versus observed capacity.
- Post-2021 fragmentation and service-delivery paragraph.

Check that formal structure is not presented as nationwide effective reach.

## Chapter 7 — 4 minutes

- Fiscal architecture paragraph based on the 2008 Constitution.
- IMF revenue, fiscal balance and debt values.
- Statement that `exp` remains non-reproducible and excluded.
- Final paragraph on current fiscal operation, SOEs and conflict finance.

Check units, years, estimates and absence of unsupported expenditure claims.

## Chapter 15 — 4 minutes

- UN credentials paragraph.
- Security-actor classification.
- ASEAN engagement paragraph.
- OCHA humanitarian quantities and population definitions.

Check recognition versus representation, and IDP/refugee/asylum-seeker separation.

## Chapter 16 — 4 minutes

- MSDP architecture and implementation matrix.
- MERP official-announcement qualification.
- FDC as interim political framework, not development plan.
- Continuity/rupture conclusion.

Check that no strategy document is treated as proof of implementation or performance.

## Chapter 17 — 6 minutes

- Authority fragmentation synthesis.
- Administrative/fiscal capacity synthesis.
- Security/service-delivery interaction.
- Strategy–implementation chain.
- Final integrated assessment.

Check that every cross-chapter conclusion is no stronger than its weakest underlying evidence.

## Visual check

- Read captions and caveats.
- Confirm `exp` is absent from publication-ready Chapter 7 charts.
- Confirm no visual implies territorial control or measured causal impact.
- Confirm source IDs and reference periods are visible in metadata.

## Final decision

- Approve for canonical integration
- Return for targeted revision
- Require further evidence collection
