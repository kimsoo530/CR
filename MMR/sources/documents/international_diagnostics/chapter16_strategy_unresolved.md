# Chapter 16 unresolved evidence

- No independently verified local copy of the full MERP text, approval instrument, implementation matrix, KPI dataset, or monitoring report was found in this focused collection. MERP is therefore cited only as an official announcement of a proposed/presented framework.
- No verified post-2021 SAC national development plan comparable in completeness to the MSDP was found in the local archive during this batch.
- No verified national implementation report establishes which MSDP action plans remained active after 2021 or which MERP action lines were implemented.
- No sufficiently documented EAO or ethnic governance development strategy was added. This is an evidence gap, not evidence that such frameworks do not exist.
- Strategy performance cannot be evaluated from the available documents alone. Existing macro-fiscal and humanitarian data may describe constraints, but do not by themselves attribute outcomes to a strategy.
- The FDC is a political/interim constitutional framework. Its references to economic, social, security and ministerial plans should not be treated as a fully costed development strategy.
