## Chapter-by-chapter review

### Chapter 3

The main legal claims are carefully qualified and the 2008 Constitution, 2021 emergency orders and competing constitutional claims are separated. The chapter should use one candidate as the integration source; the shorter “constitutional core” file should remain a focused legal supplement, not a second canonical version. Before integration, normalize article/section terminology, ensure every legal claim maps to the verified source note, and remove any conclusion stronger than the source language. Tone is academically usable after major revision.

### Chapter 5

The candidate’s strongest contribution is the distinction between formal Union/GAD machinery and effective administrative reach. The weak point is post-2021 operational evidence: formal SAC arrangements, NUG-associated claims and ethnic/local governance are not equivalent evidence. The phrase “the chapter should state” is process-facing and must be converted into reader-facing prose. The candidate needs a final source-to-claim pass and clearer reference periods.

### Chapter 7

The candidate correctly excludes the non-reproducible IMF `exp` series and treats IMF/WB data as qualified international evidence. It should not be integrated until fiscal claims, chart references and the unresolved expenditure limitation are checked together. Formal fiscal architecture is stronger than current operation, SOE evidence, audit evidence and conflict-finance evidence. The candidate is informative but still reads partly as an evidence memo.

### Chapter 15

The candidate correctly distinguishes recognition, representation, engagement and working contact, and avoids a control map. The weakest area is deliberately limited bilateral and actor coverage. Human review should confirm that each humanitarian figure retains its population definition and date, and that ASEAN/UN procedural evidence is not generalized into current diplomatic status. Major revision is required before integration.

### Chapter 16

The MSDP is a strong verified national-plan anchor. MERP is correctly restricted to an official announcement, while FDC is treated as an alternative interim political framework. The principal risk is treating framework design as implementation. Process-facing phrases such as “the candidate should” require removal, and the relationship between MSDP and MERP must remain unresolved unless new evidence is approved.

### Chapter 17

The candidate provides the best available integrated structure and preserves the qualifications of the component chapters. It does not make a composite score or nationwide control claim. Before integration, every cross-chapter assertion should be linked to the relevant chapter/source trail, transitions should be strengthened, and the wording of interaction/arrows should be confirmed as analytical rather than causal.
