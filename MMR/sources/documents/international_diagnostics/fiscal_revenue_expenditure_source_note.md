# Fiscal Revenue and Expenditure Source Note: Myanmar

- Retrieval country code: `MMR`.
- IMF DataMapper indicators: `rev` for general government revenue, `exp` for general government expenditure, and `GGXCNL_NGDP` for general government net lending/borrowing.
- World Bank WDI indicators: `GC.REV.XGRT.GD.ZS` for central government revenue excluding grants, `GC.TAX.TOTL.GD.ZS` for tax revenue, and `GC.XPN.TOTL.GD.ZS` for central government expense.
- Unit: percent of GDP.
- Editorial rule: do not merge IMF general-government and World Bank central-government series as if they had identical coverage. Use them as complementary diagnostics and triangulate with the latest national budget execution and finance ministry documents. IMF DataMapper may include estimates or projections for the most recent years, so the narrative should not present them as audited final accounts unless verified by national documents.
