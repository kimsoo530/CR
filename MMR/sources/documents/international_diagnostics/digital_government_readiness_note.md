# Digital Government Readiness Source Note: Myanmar

This section combines connectivity, secure infrastructure, governance quality, and fiscal-capacity indicators.

Core data sources:

- World Bank Indicators API: IT.NET.USER.ZS, IT.CEL.SETS.P2, IT.NET.BBND.P2, IT.NET.SECR.P6.
- Worldwide Governance Indicators: GE.EST, RQ.EST, CC.EST.
- IMF DataMapper: rev.

Raw files are archived under `MMR/raw/world_bank_digital` and `MMR/raw/imf_digital`. Processed data are stored in `MMR/processed/digital_government_readiness.csv`.
