# Chapter 7 Fiscal Architecture: Verified Source Note

Access date: 2026-08-27. Local source: `MMR/raw/official/legal/constitution_2008/constitution_2008_ministry_of_information_en_adb_archive.pdf`, 213 pages, SHA-256 `3025747B47A6F367717C581D8B9616F28448FA35449CBAD3B0D7BA07149BCD17`.

Verified provisions:

- Article 100(b), PDF p. 43: national plans, annual budgets and taxation are submitted exclusively by the Union Government to the Pyidaungsu Hluttaw.
- Article 193, PDF pp. 78-79: Region/State annual budget bills are submitted by the Region/State Government; the budget includes finance from the Union Fund and may include debt-related expenditures.
- Articles 229-230, PDF pp. 92-93: Financial Commission composition and functions, including vetting Union and Region/State budgets, recommendations concerning the Union Budget, Union Fund distributions, special grants and loans.
- Article 242, PDF p. 104: the Auditor-General of the Union is appointed to audit the Union Budget and report to the Pyidaungsu Hluttaw.
- Articles 249-253, PDF p. 110: Region/State executive and budget responsibilities.
- Articles 254-257, PDF pp. 111-112: Region/State taxes and revenues, Region/State funds, supervision of subordinate departments and the possibility of forming Region/State civil-service organizations in advance coordination with the Union Government.

Status: VERIFIED PRIMARY for constitutional fiscal architecture. These provisions establish de jure design. They do not establish current budget execution, fiscal transparency, effective intergovernmental transfers, audit follow-up or post-2021 operation.
