## Scope rule

The 2008 Constitution is the primary source for its formal legal design. The 1982 citizenship text is a verified English legal archive with translation/provenance limits. UN and International IDEA materials are used for dated interpretation and institutional conditions, never to convert actor claims or shifting governance arrangements into definitive territorial-control or recognition conclusions.
