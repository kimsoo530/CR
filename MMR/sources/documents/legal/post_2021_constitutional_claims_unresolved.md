# Post-2021 constitutional claims: unresolved items

Access date: 2026-08-26.

- The exact issuance history of the combined English *Federal Democracy Charter* PDF remains unresolved. Announcement No. 19/2021 dates the promulgation of Part I, but the PDF itself gives only the year 2021 and incorporates Part II material.
- The current CRPH Functions page says "Two months after its formation, on 5 March 2021" but does not directly establish an exact CRPH formation date. An original formation instrument is still needed before attributing 2021-02-05 as verified.
- The archive contains selected official records for the initial declaration and later extensions/transition. It does not yet contain every intervening NDSC extension order or its original signed instrument. A date-by-date exhaustive extension chronology must remain PARTIALLY VERIFIED until each underlying order is acquired and checked.
- No conclusion is made here on who is the lawful government, recognition, or the territorial reach of SAC or NUG/CRPH institutions. Those are separate legal, political and empirical questions.
