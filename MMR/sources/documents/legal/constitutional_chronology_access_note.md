# Constitutional Chronology Source Note: Myanmar

This report section is organized chronologically so that the current constitutional order is read as the result of earlier state-forming settlements, amendments, and regime transitions.

For annual updates, archive or cite the following source types before rewriting the section:

- Current constitution, basic law, or constitutional declaration.
- Historical constitutions, independence instruments, transition charters, and amendment acts.
- Official gazette, parliament, president or cabinet office, justice ministry, legal database, or constitutional court materials.
- Comparative legal compilations such as Constitute Project, CIA World Factbook background notes, and UN or regional constitutional-assistance records.
- Scholarly constitutional histories that explain why a constitutional settlement changed the political and administrative order.

The analytical text should cite the precise official source used for each major constitutional turning point whenever available.
