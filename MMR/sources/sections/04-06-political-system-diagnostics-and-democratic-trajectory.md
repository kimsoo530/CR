## 4.6 Political System Diagnostics and Democratic Trajectory

Political diagnostics should combine formal institutions with independently documented political conditions. The local archive contains indicator extracts, but the source-to-register provenance for the previously displayed V-Dem values is not sufficiently established for reader-facing quantitative claims.

The defensible conclusion is qualitative. Myanmar's political trajectory must be assessed through constitutional rules, military representation, electoral administration, executive constraints, judicial operation, and post-2021 authority claims. Aggregate indicators may provide context once their exact dataset, vintage, indicator identity, and raw provenance are registered; they cannot substitute for institutional evidence.

This section therefore records a statistical evidence gap rather than presenting an unverified democracy ranking. The formal and actor-specific evidence is developed in Chapters 3, 4, and 15.
