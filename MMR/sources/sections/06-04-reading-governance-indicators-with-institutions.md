## 6.4 Reading Governance Indicators with Institutions

Governance indicators become analytically useful when linked to institutions, mechanisms, and reference periods. For Myanmar, a WGI observation should be read alongside the constitutional rule, administrative chain, fiscal resource, security condition, or service outcome that could plausibly explain the dimension being measured (World Bank, 2025).

This approach prevents two errors. An aggregate score should not be treated as proof that every office performs similarly, and a formal institution should not be treated as effective merely because it appears in law or an organogram. Chapters 3, 5, 7, 13, and 15 supply the country-specific evidence needed to interpret the indicators.

The current series support comparative context. They do not resolve post-2021 subnational variation or establish a national causal ranking.
