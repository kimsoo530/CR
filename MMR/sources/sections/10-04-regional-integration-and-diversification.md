## 10.4 Regional Integration and Diversification

Regional integration affects Myanmar through trade corridors, border markets, infrastructure connections, labor mobility, diplomatic engagement, and access to external finance. ASEAN supplies an institutional setting, while neighboring-state relations also operate through locality-specific commercial and humanitarian channels (ASEAN, 2021; ASEAN, 2025).

Diversification requires more than new export products. It depends on customs administration, transport, electricity, payments, and predictable regulation. Conflict and uneven administrative reach can interrupt these mechanisms even when formal agreements remain in place.

The archive supports this institutional framing but does not establish a current diversification score or uniform regional-integration outcome.
