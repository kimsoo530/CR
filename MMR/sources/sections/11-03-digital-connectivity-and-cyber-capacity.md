## 11.3 Digital Connectivity and Cyber Capacity

Digital connectivity affects government communication, payments, education, health information, business continuity, and accountability. The archived World Bank indicators record internet use at 45.45 percent in 2020 and mobile subscriptions for a different reference period (World Bank, 2025). These measures show access, not affordability, reliability, security, or political openness.

Digital government capacity also depends on identity systems, data protection, interoperability, electricity, and the ability of citizens to use services. Connectivity can widen access while restrictions or outages constrain practical accountability.

The evidence supports a dated connectivity baseline. Current post-2021 network operation and cyber-governance conditions require further verified evidence.
