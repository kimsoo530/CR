## 13.1 Education System, Outcomes, and Public Spending

Education is a state function whose performance depends on schools, teachers, curriculum, finance, safety, and local access. Enrollment and public-spending indicators provide a national baseline, but they do not show whether children attend regularly, learn, or receive comparable services across conflict-affected areas (World Bank, 2025).

The formal education system must be read alongside Chapter 5's administrative reach and Chapter 15's displacement and access conditions. Budget allocation, personnel availability, transport, household displacement, and alternative providers can change effective provision without changing the formal ministry structure.

The archive supports a limited comparative baseline. Current learning outcomes, school continuity, and expenditure execution remain insufficiently observed nationally.
