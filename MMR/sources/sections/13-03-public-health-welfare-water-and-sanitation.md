## 13.3 Public Health, Welfare, Water, and Sanitation

Public health, welfare, water, and sanitation are linked through household vulnerability and local administrative capacity. Health expenditure or coverage measures do not substitute for water access, sanitation safety, food security, or social-protection receipt. Each indicator must retain its own unit, population definition, and reference year (World Health Organization, 2025; World Bank, 2025).

In Myanmar, service continuity depends on infrastructure, local personnel, humanitarian coordination, household displacement, and the authority able to operate in a given locality. Formal programmes may coexist with community, humanitarian, or actor-specific provision. These channels should not be aggregated into a single national welfare system.

The archive supports a national baseline but not a complete post-2021 locality-level welfare account.
