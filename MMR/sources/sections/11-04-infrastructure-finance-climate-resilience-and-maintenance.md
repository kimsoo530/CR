## 11.4 Infrastructure Finance, Climate Resilience, and Maintenance

Infrastructure performance depends on planning, financing, procurement, construction, maintenance, and local operation. In Myanmar, fiscal pressure and administrative fragmentation can affect each stage differently. A formally approved project does not prove completion, and a completed asset does not prove reliable service.

Climate and disaster exposure add a resilience requirement for roads, power, water, schools, and clinics. The present archive does not contain a verified national asset-condition or project-execution dataset that would support a resilience ranking.

The strongest current conclusion is institutional: infrastructure policy must be evaluated through finance, administrative reach, maintenance capacity, and service continuity together.
