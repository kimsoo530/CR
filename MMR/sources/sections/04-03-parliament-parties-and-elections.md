## 4.3 Parliament, Parties, and Elections

Parliamentary and electoral institutions in Myanmar must be assessed through both constitutional rules and the conditions of political competition. The Constitution establishes the two Union Hluttaws, Region and State Hluttaws, electoral administration, and reserved Defence Services representation (Republic of the Union of Myanmar, 2008).

The existence of electoral procedures does not establish competitive choice. Campaign access, party registration, media conditions, voter participation, dispute resolution, security, and acceptance of results determine whether representation operates as a meaningful accountability mechanism. These conditions changed sharply after 2021, but the local archive does not support a complete current assessment for every locality.

The evidence therefore supports a distinction between formal representative institutions and observed political competition.
