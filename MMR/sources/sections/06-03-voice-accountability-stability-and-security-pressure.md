## 6.3 Voice, Accountability, Stability, and Security Pressure

Voice, accountability, political stability, and security indicators describe broad conditions in which public institutions operate. They should not be read as a direct count of rights, territorial control, or service performance. In Myanmar, constitutional rights, political representation, security conditions, and administrative reach are analytically related but empirically distinct (World Bank, 2025).

The post-2021 environment makes this distinction essential. External indicators can signal severe pressure while remaining unable to identify variation among localities or competing authorities. Security pressure may restrict movement and administration, but the mechanism and effect require dated evidence.

The indicators are therefore contextual diagnostics, not a composite governance score.
