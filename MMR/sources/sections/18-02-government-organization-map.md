## 18.2 Government Organization Map

The formal organization map is a dated de jure structure. At Union level it includes the President, Union Government, ministries, the Union Civil Service Board, the Auditor-General, the Central Bank, and other constitutional bodies. The constitutional security architecture separately places the Commander-in-Chief and Defence Services within the National Defence and Security Council and provides specific appointment channels for Defence, Home Affairs, and Border Affairs.

Below the Union level, the formal territorial chain runs through Regions or States, Union Territories, districts, townships, wards, village-tracts, and constitutionally recognized Self-Administered Areas. These administrative layers must be distinguished from elected Hluttaws and from observed local operation.

The map is not a current control map. Post-2021 administrative reach varies by locality and period, and competing authorities or service providers may operate alongside or outside the formal chain.
