The administrative system of Myanmar is analyzed as an operating machinery rather than a list of ministries. The chapter separates organization, personnel, finance, territorial administration, digital government, and reform implementation because each component creates a different kind of state capacity. Recruitment, training, promotion, performance appraisal, retirement, pensions, and professional cadres should be studied with national civil service laws, payroll data, public employment statistics, and administrative reform reports.
