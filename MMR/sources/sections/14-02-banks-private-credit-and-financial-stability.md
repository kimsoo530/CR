## 14.2 Banks, Private Credit, and Financial Stability

Banks connect savings, payments, firms, households, and public finance. Private-credit indicators can show formal intermediation depth, but they do not measure credit quality, non-performing loans, informal finance, or access across regions. The archived World Bank series is a dated national signal rather than a complete post-2021 banking balance sheet (World Bank, 2025).

Financial stability depends on regulation, liquidity, foreign-exchange exposure, depositor confidence, and the capacity of banks and firms to operate under uncertainty. These mechanisms affect public-sector payments and private investment. A formal regulatory framework should therefore be separated from observed market functioning.

Current bank-level and locality-level evidence is not sufficiently verified for a full stability diagnosis.
