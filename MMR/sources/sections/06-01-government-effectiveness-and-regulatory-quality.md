## 6.1 Government Effectiveness and Regulatory Quality

Government effectiveness and regulatory quality are aggregate governance signals, not direct measures of whether a ministry or township office performs its functions. The World Bank indicators summarize perceptions and expert assessments across broad dimensions, while Chapters 3 and 5 identify the formal institutions and administrative chains behind those outcomes (World Bank, 2025).

For Myanmar, interpretation requires attention to personnel, fiscal resources, information, security, and locality-specific access. A low aggregate score can indicate broad institutional constraint, but it cannot distinguish formal capacity from effective operation in every area or identify the causal contribution of conflict, policy, or administration.

The indicators are therefore used as context for institutional analysis rather than as a composite national capacity score.
