## 18.5 Update Workflow and Evidence Archive

Updates begin with the source register and evidence plan, followed by verification of raw files before reader-facing prose changes. Legal and official actor documents are checked for issuer, date, document number, language, and relevant provision. Statistical series retain indicator ID, unit, year range, raw path, processed path, and caveat. Visuals are inserted only when their source basis and reference period are recorded.

The canonical sections remain the reproducible working layer, while generated HTML is a reader-facing output. This separation allows source provenance, citation links, asset paths, navigation, and integrated quality checks to be reviewed independently.
