## 10.3 FDI, Investment Climate, and Industrial Policy

Foreign direct investment can connect external finance to infrastructure, industrial capacity, employment, and technology. The World Bank archive provides aggregate FDI indicators, but those figures do not show project quality, sectoral distribution, ownership, reinvestment, or geographic reach (World Bank, 2025).

Myanmar's investment climate is shaped by macroeconomic instability, foreign-exchange constraints, conflict risk, administrative predictability, sanctions exposure, and access to energy and transport. Industrial policy therefore depends on the administrative and fiscal conditions described in Chapters 5 and 7. An official policy announcement is evidence of intended direction, not proof of implementation or investment response.

The available evidence supports a mechanism-based account of investment constraints. Current project-level and post-2021 investment data remain limited.
