## 14.4 Financial Regulation and Investor Protection

Financial regulation determines who may provide services, how risks are monitored, how disputes are handled, and whether investors and depositors can rely on enforcement. Formal laws and mandates establish a de jure framework; they do not demonstrate effective supervision or consistent remedies.

In Myanmar, regulatory capacity is connected to administrative personnel, court access, foreign-exchange rules, banking stability, and institutional credibility. Investor protection and financial inclusion are distinct: access can expand without strong disclosure or enforcement.

The local archive contains broad international indicators but no verified current dataset on inspections, enforcement actions, complaints, or investor losses. A complete post-2021 regulatory-performance assessment remains unresolved.
