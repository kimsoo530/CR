## 11.1 Electricity and Energy Security

Electricity is both an economic input and a condition of administrative reach. Reliable power supports hospitals, schools, water systems, firms, telecommunications, and government offices. The World Bank archive includes access-to-electricity data, but the national indicator does not identify reliability, regional variation, or post-2021 service continuity (World Bank, 2025).

Myanmar's energy system also connects public finance, resource governance, infrastructure investment, and external relations. Formal energy institutions and projects must therefore be distinguished from effective supply at the locality level.

The current evidence supports a national access baseline, not a grid-performance ranking or conflict-area map. Dated regional supply, outage, generation, and financing evidence remains needed.
