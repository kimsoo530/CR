## 17.4 Contribution to Comparative Administration Theory

The integrated assessment interprets Myanmar as a comparative-administration case. It identifies the country's operating model, strengths, bottlenecks, cooperation opportunities, and theoretical contribution to debates about state capacity, political settlement, development strategy, and administrative reform. The case should be used to test whether common theories about developmental states, neopatrimonialism, rentier administration, dominant-party rule, federalism, fragility, decentralization, or bureaucratic professionalism explain the observed pattern.
