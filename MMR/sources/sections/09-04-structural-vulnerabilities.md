## 9.4 External Position, Trade Balance, and Structural Constraints

Myanmar's external position determines whether the economy can finance imports, investment, technology, and debt service. The archived trade data provide merchandise export, import, and balance measures for 2024, while current-account and broader goods-and-services series have earlier or incomplete coverage (World Bank, 2025). These periods cannot be combined into one seamless post-2021 narrative.

External vulnerability operates through imported inputs, export concentration, foreign-exchange availability, border-trade conditions, remittance channels, and international payments. These mechanisms affect fiscal administration and service delivery as well as private investment.

The evidence supports identifying exposure and data breaks, not attributing every change to sanctions, conflict, or a single external actor. The current archive is strongest for descriptive merchandise-trade indicators.
