## 13.4 Human Development and Territorial Equity

Human development is uneven when people have different access to income, education, health, safety, infrastructure, and public authority. National averages may conceal regional, ethnic, urban-rural, displacement, and conflict-related differences. Myanmar's formal territorial hierarchy therefore needs to be read together with service data and administrative reach.

The available international indicators provide comparable outcome signals, while the local archive has limited verified subnational series. It would be unsafe to convert national indicators into a current territorial-equity ranking or infer that formal boundaries correspond to equal service provision.

A defensible assessment identifies the mechanisms of inequality and records measurement limits. Current cross-locality comparisons remain unresolved.
