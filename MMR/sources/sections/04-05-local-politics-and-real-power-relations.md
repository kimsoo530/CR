Local politics in Myanmar links the national political order to regions, municipalities, districts, villages, and community institutions. The central question is whether local leaders have electoral legitimacy, fiscal discretion, and administrative authority, or whether they mainly transmit centrally defined priorities. Local politics also affects party organization, patronage, public employment, social assistance, land administration, policing, and the territorial distribution of public investment.
