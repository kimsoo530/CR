## 12.2 Women, Youth, Vulnerable Employment, and Skills

Labor-market opportunity is distributed by gender, age, location, education, and migration status. Female participation and youth employment indicators can identify broad constraints, but they do not measure unpaid care, unsafe work, displacement, or training access without complementary evidence (International Labour Organization, 2025).

For Myanmar, skills policy is tied to education continuity, migration, household coping, and employer demand. Young people outside stable schooling or employment may face long-term human-capital losses, while women may bear additional care and mobility constraints. These mechanisms require evidence rather than assumptions from aggregate rates.

The archive provides comparable labor indicators but limited post-2021 subnational and conflict-sensitive coverage. A complete vulnerability assessment remains unresolved.
