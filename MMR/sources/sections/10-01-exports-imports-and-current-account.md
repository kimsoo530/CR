## 10.1 Exports, Imports, and Current Account

Myanmar's external accounts connect domestic production to foreign-exchange earnings and import capacity. The local World Bank archive contains merchandise trade indicators for 2024, while broader goods-and-services and current-account series have different coverage periods and missing observations (World Bank, 2025). These measures must remain separate.

Exports provide foreign exchange, while imports supply fuel, machinery, medicine, food, and industrial inputs. The resulting external position affects firms, public procurement, household prices, and fiscal adjustment. A merchandise surplus cannot be treated as a complete current-account balance because services, income, and transfers are measured separately.

The evidence supports a mechanism-based external-sector account. It does not establish a complete post-2021 balance-of-payments adjustment or attribute changes to one actor without harmonized, dated evidence.
