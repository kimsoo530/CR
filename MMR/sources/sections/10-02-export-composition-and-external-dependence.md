## 10.2 Export Composition and External Dependence

Export dependence is shaped by the products Myanmar sells, the markets and corridors through which they move, and the amount of domestic value added retained. The current archive provides aggregate merchandise indicators but no verified post-2021 product-level register sufficient to measure concentration by sector or destination.

A large export value can coexist with narrow product concentration, border-corridor exposure, or limited employment. Agricultural and resource exports may generate foreign exchange without producing broad-based income or predictable public revenue. These effects require product, firm, and fiscal evidence rather than inference from aggregate trade totals.

The present evidence supports an analytical framework for external dependence. Precise current export concentration remains unresolved.
