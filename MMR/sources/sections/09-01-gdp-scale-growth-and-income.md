## 9.1 GDP Scale, Growth, and Income Level

Myanmar's macroeconomic baseline should separate economic scale, real growth, and household income. The archived World Bank series records 2024 GDP at approximately US$74.07 billion and GDP per capita at approximately US$1,359 in current dollars (World Bank, 2025). These measures describe scale and nominal income level; they do not establish living standards, purchasing power, or income distribution.

GDP indicates the potential tax and foreign-exchange base, while real growth indicates whether that base is expanding or contracting. GDP per capita places the development challenge closer to households, but current-dollar measures remain sensitive to exchange rates and should not be read as constant-price welfare measures.

The available series provide a comparable statistical spine. Post-2021 causal attribution and distributional effects remain incompletely observed.
