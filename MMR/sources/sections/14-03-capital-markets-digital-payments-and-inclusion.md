## 14.3 Capital Markets, Digital Payments, and Inclusion

Capital markets and digital payments can widen access to savings, transfers, and investment, but their reach depends on regulation, electricity, connectivity, identity systems, trust, and protection from fraud or exclusion. Mobile and internet indicators provide access baselines, not proof that households can use secure financial services (World Bank, 2025).

Myanmar's digital-finance question is institutional. Payment channels may support remittances and commerce while restrictions, outages, or limited interoperability constrain reliability. Capital-market activity remains distinct from mobile-money inclusion and should not be treated as one measure of financial development.

The evidence supports a mechanism-based account. Comprehensive post-2021 usage and inclusion data remain incomplete.
