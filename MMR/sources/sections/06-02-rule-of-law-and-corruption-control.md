## 6.2 Rule of Law and Corruption Control

Rule-of-law and corruption indicators provide comparable signals about legal predictability and control of public power, but they do not replace constitutional, judicial, or administrative evidence. Myanmar's formal legal order and accountability institutions must be assessed separately from whether citizens can obtain remedies or whether rules are applied consistently (World Bank, 2025).

Corruption control is connected to procurement, revenue, personnel, courts, audit, and local discretion. The same indicator may reflect several mechanisms and cannot establish that a particular office or actor is responsible for a national result.

The current archive supports cautious comparative interpretation. It does not provide a complete post-2021 locality-level rule-of-law or corruption account.
