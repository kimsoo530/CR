## 4.2 Executive Power and Government Formation

Executive power in Myanmar is shaped by the relationship among the President, Union Government, security institutions, ministries, and emergency provisions. The 2008 constitutional design assigns formal executive responsibilities while also giving the Commander-in-Chief a constitutionally specified role in security and emergency arrangements (Republic of the Union of Myanmar, 2008).

After February 2021, official SAC records, CRPH/NUG instruments, and external assessments describe different authority frameworks. These documents should be distinguished by issuer and status. An official claim about government formation does not by itself establish effective nationwide coordination, recognition, or implementation.

The political question is therefore how authorization moves from formal institutions to ministries, local administration, budgets, and public services under contested conditions.
