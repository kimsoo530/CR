## 12.4 Education, TVET, Innovation, and Human Capital

Human capital connects education systems to labor productivity, public-sector staffing, and long-term development strategy. Enrollment indicators show participation but not learning quality, completion, safety, or access during conflict. The archive provides selected education and labor measures, while current locality-level training and learning evidence is limited (World Bank, 2025).

Technical and vocational education is relevant to industrial diversification and administrative reconstruction. Its effectiveness depends on stable institutions, qualified instructors, equipment, electricity, employer links, and recognized credentials. A strategy document can state these goals without demonstrating implementation.

The evidence supports an institutional human-capital diagnosis. Comparable outcome measures and post-2021 coverage remain uneven.
