## 13.2 Health Outcomes and Service Capacity

Health outcomes reflect service inputs and the conditions in which people seek care. Life expectancy, infant mortality, hospital beds, and health expenditure provide comparable indicators in the archived WHO and World Bank materials, but their reference years and coverage differ (World Health Organization, 2025; World Bank, 2025).

Myanmar's health capacity is mediated by staffing, medicines, facilities, transport, security, and humanitarian access. A formal health ministry or national indicator cannot establish uniform service availability. Conflict and displacement may shift care toward local providers, civil-society systems, or humanitarian channels that national statistics do not capture.

The evidence supports a systems diagnosis with explicit year and population definitions. Current subnational service capacity remains an evidence gap.
