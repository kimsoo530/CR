## 9.3 Sectoral Structure, Resource Dependence, and Growth Model

Myanmar's archived 2024 value-added shares were approximately 20.8 percent for agriculture, 37.8 percent for industry, and 41.4 percent for services (World Bank, 2025). These shares describe recorded output, not employment quality, productivity distribution, or fiscal contribution.

Agriculture remains closely connected to rural livelihoods and local infrastructure. Industry includes manufacturing, construction, energy, and extraction whose policy and fiscal effects differ. Services may represent formal business, public services, trade, finance, or low-productivity informal work. Sector shares therefore require labor, trade, and fiscal evidence before they are interpreted as a development model.

The data support a broad structural description, but not a current estimate of resource rents, SOE exposure, or conflict-linked extraction.
